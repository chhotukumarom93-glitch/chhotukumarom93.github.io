Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Yes, all our tools including the Image Compressor and PDF Converter are 100% free with no hidden charges or registration required.

Simply select the 'Image Compressor' tool, upload your file, enter '50' in the Target Size box, and click Process. The tool will handle the rest.
