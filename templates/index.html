The Smartest Way to Compress Images Online

Photo Tools Pro is a professional-grade image optimization suite designed to help you balance file size and visual quality. Our platform uses advanced lossy and lossless compression algorithms to ensure that your photos meet the strict requirements of online application portals without becoming blurry or pixelated.

Why Compress Photos?

Most government portals (like UPSC, SSC, Banking) require images under 20KB or 50KB. Uploading a high-resolution 5MB photo is impossible. Our tool intelligently shrinks the data footprint of your JPG or PNG files while preserving the details of your face and signature.

How it Works?

When you upload a file, our AI analyzes the pixels. It identifies areas where data can be optimized without human eyes noticing a difference. You can specify the exact KB size you need, and our system will recalculate the quality to match your target perfectly.

Key Features of Photo Tools Pro:

  • Batch Conversion: Convert multiple images to PDF in one go.
  • Passport Ready: Automatically adjust dimensions to 3.5cm x 4.5cm for official Indian ID standards.
  • Instant Background Removal: Our AI removes backgrounds with 99% accuracy for professional profile pictures.
  • Privacy First: We do not store your images. All files are processed and then permanently deleted from our server cache.

Step-by-Step Guide to Use Photo Tools Pro

  1. Upload: Click on the upload box and select the image from your PC or Mobile.
  2. Select Tool: Choose whether you want to compress, resize, or remove the background.
  3. Set Parameters: If you are compressing, enter the target size (e.g., 50KB).

What is the best format for online forms?

Most portals prefer JPG/JPEG for photos and signatures. For documents like certificates, PDF is the recommended format. Our tool supports all these conversions.
